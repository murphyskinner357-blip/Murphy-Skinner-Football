2004 Hardwood Classic — Patterson vs. St. Stanislaus

An archived newspaper report dated Tuesday, December 28, 2004 documents Patterson's first-round Hardwood Classic game in Gulfport, Mississippi.

  • Murphy Skinner: 13 points
  • Raylon May: 17 points
  • Warrell Span: 15 points
  • Patterson advanced in the winner's bracket to face Archbishop Rummel.

The story states Patterson led 31–24 at halftime, was outscored 16–10 in the third quarter, and closed with a 24–8 fourth quarter. Those figures produce a 65–48 Patterson victory.

Source status: The publication date is visible in the recovered Newspapers.com screenshot. The publication name and page number are cropped, so the archive does not guess them.

All-Tournament note: This report verifies Skinner's 13-point first-round performance, but it does not itself verify the All-Tournament Team selection. That honor remains a separate source target.
