February 7, 2005 — Patterson 66, West St. Mary 59

Source: The Daily Review, Monday, February 7, 2005, page 9. Article: “'Jacks take over first in 7-3A”.

Verified facts: Murphy Skinner and Warrell Span scored 20 points apiece. Skinner made two three-pointers. Patterson won 66–59, improved to 21–8 overall and 4–1 in District 7-3A, and moved into first place in the district.
