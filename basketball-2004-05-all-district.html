2004–05 First-Team All-District 7-3A Basketball

Murphy Skinner, a senior starter for Patterson High School, was voted First-Team All-District 7-3A in basketball for the 2004–05 season.

Verified team context

  • Patterson placed three players on the All-District first team: Murphy Skinner, Raylon May and Warrell Span.
  • Warrell Span was named the district MVP.
  • Patterson won the District 7-3A championship.
  • Patterson advanced to the Louisiana Class 3A state quarterfinals.
  • Patterson head coach Lorenzo Wright was named District Coach of the Year.

Source status

This honor is verified by an archived newspaper article titled “Jacks load players”. The recovered clipping states that each of Patterson's five senior starters earned a spot on the 2004–05 District 7-3A team and specifically lists Murphy Skinner among the first-team selections. Publication date and page number are still being documented from the archive.
