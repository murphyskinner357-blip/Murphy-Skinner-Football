Who is Murphy Skinner?

Murphy Skinner played varsity football and basketball for Patterson High School in Patterson, Louisiana. In football he played the 2002, 2003 and 2004 seasons, wore jersey No. 26 and played wide receiver and defensive back.

After graduating from Patterson in 2005, Skinner continued his football career at Pasadena City College in California. The 2005 Lancer roster lists him as a freshman wide receiver, No. 84, at 6'0" and 207 pounds, from Patterson, Louisiana.

Head coach at Patterson football: Tommy Minton

Quarterbacks during his Patterson football career: Coe Boudreaux, Brock Landry, Tremaine Vaughns and Corey Hilliard.

2003 quarterbacks:

  • Tremaine Vaughns — senior starting quarterback; played through two severely sprained ankles early in the season, then suffered a cracked collarbone against Westlake and ultimately missed six starts.
  • Corey Hilliard — freshman quarterback (2003); received early varsity action and was called on for substantial playing time while Vaughns was injured.

College: Pasadena City College Lancers, 2005 freshman football season.
