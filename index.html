Who is Murphy Skinner?

Murphy Skinner played varsity football for Patterson High School in Patterson, Louisiana, during the 2002, 2003 and 2004 seasons. He wore jersey No. 26 and played wide receiver and defensive back.

After graduating from Patterson in 2005, Skinner continued his football career at Pasadena City College in California. The 2005 Lancer roster lists him as a freshman wide receiver, No. 84, at 6'0" and 207 pounds, from Patterson, Louisiana.

Head coach at Patterson: Tommy Minton

Quarterbacks during his Patterson career: Coe Boudreaux, Brock Landry, Tremane Vaughns and Corey Hilliard.

2003 quarterbacks:

  • Tremane Vaughns — senior quarterback; dealt with injuries during the season.
  • Corey Hilliard — freshman quarterback (2003); also played quarterback during Skinner's junior season.

College: Pasadena City College Lancers, 2005 freshman season.
