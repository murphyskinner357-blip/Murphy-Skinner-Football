Who is Murphy Skinner?

Murphy Skinner played varsity football for Patterson High School in Patterson, Louisiana, during the 2002, 2003 and 2004 seasons. He wore jersey No. 26 and played wide receiver and defensive back.

His documented varsity career began during his sophomore season in 2002—not his freshman year—and continued through his senior season in 2004.

Head coach: Tommy Minton

Quarterbacks during his career: Coe Boudreaux, Brock Landry, Tremaine Vaughns and Corey Hilliard.

2003 quarterback context: During Skinner's junior season, senior Tremaine Vaughns and freshman Corey Hilliard both played quarterback. Vaughns dealt with injuries during the season.

Final high school game: Patterson's first-round Class 3A playoff game against St. Charles Catholic in 2004.
